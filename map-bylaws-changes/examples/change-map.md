# Change Map (example)
An abridged, genericized example that pairs with the [Initial Translation example](initial-translation.md). Each governing clause from the Initial Translation is followed by its disposition and its destination or reason. The original hierarchy is retained; bracketed editorial notes are omitted. "Bylaws" references point to the proposed bylaws, and module references point to proposed rule modules such as Financial stewardship. A full Change Map continues through every section and then reproduces the proposed bylaws, marking new and changed rules relative to the translation.

## 1. Purpose and Powers
1. The Network is organized for charitable and educational purposes under Section 501(c)(3). *[Cited upward → Articles 3(A), 3(D)–(E); summarized in Bylaws 1.1.1–1.1.5.]*
2. It supports its member communities by connecting them with resources and with each other. *[Cited upward → Articles 3(A), 3(D)–(E); summarized in Bylaws 1.1.1–1.1.5.]*
3. Its purposes are defined more fully in Article 3(A) of the Articles of Incorporation. *[Cited upward → Articles 3(A), 3(D)–(E); summarized in Bylaws 1.1.1–1.1.5.]*
4. The Board adopts the Network's Mission and Values Statement. *[Moved to operational practice. Mission and values statements fall within ordinary Board operational authority under Bylaws 4.1.1 and may be assigned under 5.2; no separate governing statement is required.]*
    1. Until the first Assembly, only the Board may amend it. *[Removed as spent. The first Assembly has already occurred.]*
    2. After the first Assembly, only the Assembly may amend it. *[Intentionally removed. The Assembly no longer has exclusive authority over a Mission and Values Statement; such statements are operational work under Bylaws 4.1.1 and 5.2.]*
    3. The Mission and Values Statement must reflect and state the purposes defined in Article 3(A) of the Articles of Incorporation. *[Intentionally removed. The Mission and Values Statement need not repeat the Articles' purposes; Bylaws 1.1 summarizes them directly.]*
    4. Member organization MOUs must incorporate the Mission and Values Statement. *[Intentionally removed → Bylaws 2.1.6 replaces separate copied membership terms with a signed acceptance of the official bylaws.]*
5. The Mission and Values Statement guides decisions by the Board, Officers, and members, but does not change any rights, responsibilities, or procedures established by these Bylaws or the Articles of Incorporation. *[Moved to operational practice. Mission and values may guide the Network's work without becoming binding membership terms or a separate governing document.]*
6. In any conflict, the Articles of Incorporation control over these Bylaws, and these Bylaws control over the Mission and Values Statement. *[Cited upward → the state nonprofit corporation act governs the relationship between the Articles and bylaws. Mission and values statements are operational documents.]*

## 3. Fiscal and Legal
### 3.1 Fiscal Year
1. The Network's fiscal year runs from October 1 through September 30. *[Carried into Financial stewardship module 1.1.]*

### 3.2 Fidelity Bonds
1. The Network must maintain fidelity bonds covering Board-appointed officers, employees, and agents who handle the Network's funds, property, or other assets. *[Intentionally removed → Financial stewardship module 5.1–5.2 replaces compulsory fidelity bonds with a recorded insurance decision and actual external requirements.]*
2. The Board approves the form and amount of this coverage. *[Carried into Financial stewardship module 5.1–5.2.]*
3. The bonds must protect the Network against losses caused by fraud, theft, misappropriation, or other dishonest acts. *[Intentionally removed → Financial stewardship module 5.1–5.2 replaces compulsory fidelity bonds with a recorded insurance decision and actual external requirements.]*
4. Any money or property recovered after a loss must be returned to the Network entity or entities that suffered the loss. *[Consolidated, with changes → Financial stewardship module 5.3. Allocation follows actual entitlement rather than an unexplained set of Network entities.]*
5. If more than one Network entity suffered the loss and the Network does not recover the full amount, the recovery must be divided among them in proportion to their losses. *[Consolidated, with changes → Financial stewardship module 5.3. Allocation follows actual entitlement rather than an unexplained set of Network entities.]*

### 3.3 Bank Accounts and Depositories
1. The Board selects the financial institutions that hold the Network's funds, property, and other assets. *[Consolidated → Financial stewardship module 1.3.]*
2. The Board designates, by title, who is responsible for controlling those assets. *[Consolidated → Bylaws 6.2.1 and Financial stewardship module 2.6. Authority is recorded for named people rather than inferred from titles.]*

### 3.4 Checking, Savings, and Depository Accounts
1. All cash received by the Network must be deposited into the designated General Fund checking account. *[Intentionally removed → Financial stewardship module 1.3 requires Network accounts without requiring a particular General Fund account.]*
2. Money may be withdrawn from that account only by check or letter of transmittal signed by any two Officers (the President, Vice President, Secretary, or Treasurer) or their designees. *[Consolidated, with changes → Financial stewardship module 2.1–2.6. Retain two-person approval by default and allow recorded controls for electronic payments.]*
3. The Board must approve any transfer of other Network assets or property between financial institutions. *[Consolidated → Bylaws 6.2.1. Transfers use the same recorded financial authority as other transactions.]*

### 3.5 Budget
1. The Treasurer prepares the Network's budget. *[Carried into Financial stewardship module 4.8 and Bylaws 6.1.1, with responsibility for submission assigned to the Board rather than requiring one budget author.]*
2. The Finance Committee may review the proposed budget. *[Intentionally removed. Reviewing a proposal does not need a special permission or permanent committee.]*
3. The Treasurer submits the proposed budget to the Board for review and revision. *[Consolidated → Bylaws 6.1.1. The Board submits the budget to the Assembly.]*
4. The Board recommends a budget to the Assembly. *[Consolidated → Bylaws 6.1.1. The Board submits the budget to the Assembly.]*
5. Once the Assembly has been established, the Treasurer must provide the budget to the Assembly at least 15 days before the annual Assembly. *[Carried, with changed timing → Bylaws 6.1.2. Keep 15 days of review without tying approval to the annual event.]*
6. The Assembly approves the budget at the annual meeting. *[Carried, with changed process → Bylaws 6.1.3 and 3.2. Budget approval uses a written vote.]*
7. Any delegate may propose an amendment to the budget. *[Consolidated → Bylaws 3.2.3 and 3.2.7. Member organization delegates can propose a revised budget before voting; changed text requires a fresh ballot.]*
    1. If another delegate seconds the proposal, the Assembly may vote on it. *[Intentionally removed. No motion or second is needed to submit a written proposal.]*

### 3.6 Contracts
1. The Network's Counsel must review every contract and approve its legal form before the Network signs it. *[Intentionally removed in part → Financial stewardship module 3.2 retains default counsel review above $2,500; universal review of every contract is removed.]*
2. Contracts valued at $2,500 or less may be signed by staff authorized by both the President and Secretary. *[Carried into Financial stewardship module 3.3, with an express ability for the Board to replace the delegation.]*
3. Contracts valued above $2,500 must be signed by both the President and Secretary. *[Carried into Financial stewardship module 3.2, with an express ability for the Board to replace the delegation.]*

### 3.7 Conflict of Interest
1. The Board must maintain a conflict of interest policy. *[Consolidated, with changes → Bylaws 5.4.1–5.4.3 states disclosure, nonparticipation and Director conflict-transaction approval directly; Financial stewardship module 3.4 retains the supporting record.]*
2. Officers, Directors, key employees, and anyone else designated by the Board must follow that policy. *[Consolidated, with changes → Bylaws 5.4.1–5.4.3 applies concrete conflict safeguards to everyone exercising Board authority.]*

### 3.8 Compensation
1. The President, Vice President, and Treasurer must approve compensation for all paid Network personnel. *[Intentionally removed → Bylaws 5.2 and 6.2.1 use recorded authority for staff pay instead of requiring three particular officers to approve it.]*
2. The Board must approve compensation for any Board-appointed officer. *[Consolidated → Bylaws 5.4.6.]*
3. Compensation approved for a Board-appointed officer must be published on the Network's website. *[Carried → Bylaws 5.4.7.]*
